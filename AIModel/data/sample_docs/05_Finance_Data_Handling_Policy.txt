FINANCE DEPARTMENT DATA HANDLING AND RECORDS POLICY
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0 | Effective: April 2024 | Review: April 2025

1. PURPOSE
This document describes how the Finance Department of Nexacore Technologies Sdn Bhd manages financial records, vendor payments, payroll data, and related documentation.

2. FINANCIAL DATA MANAGED
The Finance Department manages: employee payroll records, vendor payment records, customer invoice records, internal budget reports, tax filings, and financial statements.

3. PAYMENT AUTHORISATION CONTROLS
3.1 Vendor payments above LKR 500,000 require dual authorisation from two Finance officers.
3.2 New vendor bank details must be verified by telephone callback before processing.
3.3 Changes to vendor bank details require Finance Manager approval in writing.
3.4 Urgent payments require written CFO approval before processing.

4. PAYROLL PROCESSING
Monthly payroll is processed by the designated payroll officer. Pay slips are sent to employees via email. Payroll errors must be reported to the Finance Manager within 3 business days. Payroll files are transmitted to the bank for salary crediting.

5. RECORD KEEPING
All financial records are kept for 7 years in accordance with statutory requirements. Records are stored in shared Finance network drives accessible to Finance staff. Physical records are stored in locked filing cabinets in the Finance office.

6. VENDOR MANAGEMENT
Vendor bank details and contact information are maintained in the accounting system. New vendors are added to the approved vendor register after Finance Manager sign-off.

7. AUDIT AND REVIEW
Annual external audits are conducted by the appointed external auditor. Internal audit reviews are conducted quarterly to check payment authorisation compliance.

8. POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by the Finance Director.
