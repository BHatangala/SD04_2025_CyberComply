THIRD-PARTY VENDOR MANAGEMENT POLICY
Department: Procurement / Information Technology
Version: 1.0 | Effective: January 2024 | Review: January 2025

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes the process for selecting, engaging, and managing third-party vendors and suppliers that provide goods and services to Nexacore Technologies Sdn Bhd.

2. VENDOR ONBOARDING
2.1 New vendor requests are submitted to Procurement using the Vendor Request Form.
2.2 Procurement obtains vendor business registration documents and bank details.
2.3 Finance approves the vendor for payment processing.
2.4 Approved vendors are added to the approved vendor register.

3. VENDOR CONTRACTS
All vendor engagements are formalised through a purchase order or service agreement. Contracts are reviewed by the Legal Department for commercial terms including payment schedules, deliverables, penalties, and termination clauses.

4. VENDOR SYSTEM ACCESS
Where vendors require system access, IT grants access upon department head request. Vendor accounts are tracked in the IT asset management system and removed when the vendor engagement ends.

5. ONGOING VENDOR MANAGEMENT
Procurement manages active vendor relationships and handles contract renewals. Annual performance reviews cover delivery, quality, and contractual compliance.

6. VENDOR TERMINATION
When a vendor engagement ends: Procurement and IT are notified, final invoices are processed, the vendor is marked inactive in the register, and IT removes system access.

7. POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by the Procurement Director.
